Three Instagram and Facebook layouts for TropiFix: a tip post, a job done post and a request story. Build every post from one of them.

**What you provide:** a headline of 12 words or fewer, one or two sentences of body text, the service name and area, and for the job done post a real photo of finished work.

**Tip post (1080 × 1080).** Use `sand` for the ground, a `teal-tint` chip with the service icon, an `h1`-size headline in `teal-deep` and body text in `ink`. Add one crossed-pill mark in `coral` bleeding off the top-right corner, and nothing else in coral. Put `tropifix-transparent.png` bottom-left and @tropifixcr bottom-right in `ink-muted`.

**Job done post (1080 × 1080).** The photo fills the top 62% with no rounded corners. Below it is a `teal` band with a small all-caps line (service · area), the headline in `on-teal` and the tagline. Put `tropifix-white.png` bottom-right.

**Request story (1080 × 1920).** Use a `teal` ground, `tropifix-white.png` top-left, the headline and body in `on-teal`, one `coral` pill button with `ink` text, and the website underneath. Keep all text out of the top 250px and bottom 340px, where Instagram puts its own buttons.

**Rules for every template**
- Keep a margin of at least 64px at 1080px wide.
- Use one coral element per post at most.
- Headlines are in Poppins, body text in Nunito Sans, all in sentence case.
- Use no emoji in the artwork. One is allowed in the caption.
- Don't put the transparent logo on teal or on a photo. Use the white logo there.
- Spanish posts get their own artwork with the headline written in Spanish (usted), not a subtitle under the English.
